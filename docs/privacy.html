Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pump Rise: Random ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent experience while using our application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Pump Rise: Random is designed with privacy as a priority:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • Local Processing: All core functionalities of the app, including song list generation and league selection, happen entirely on your device.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or sign in to use the App.

2. Third-Party Services

While we do not collect your data directly, the App interacts with the following third-party services to provide specific features:

  • YouTube Integration: When you search for a song, the App opens a query in your device’s default browser or the YouTube app. Your interaction with YouTube is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: As an Android application, the App may use standard Google Play Services for stability and diagnostics. These services are governed by the Google Play Services Privacy Policy.

3. Data Storage

All data related to your training sessions and the song database is bundled within the App's local storage. We do not use external servers to store your session history or preferences.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

6. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us through the official developer channels on the Google Play Store.
